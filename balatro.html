📖 How to Play

🎯 Goal

Beat each blind by reaching the target score before running out of hands. Progress through rounds to win!

🖱️ Controls

Click cards to select up to 5, then press Play Hand to score or Discard to swap them.

📊 Scoring

Each hand has base Chips and Mult. Your score is Chips × Mult. Jokers and enhancements boost these values!

🃏 Jokers

Start with 2 jokers (up to 5-6 total). Buy more in the shop after each blind. Jokers have different rarities:

  • Common - Basic bonuses
  • Uncommon - Better effects
  • Rare - Powerful multipliers
  • Legendary - Game-changing abilities

✨ Card Enhancements

  • Foil - Shimmering effect, +50 chips
  • Holo - Rainbow colors, +10 mult
  • Steel - Metallic, ×1.5 mult

🔮 Consumables

Tarots - One-time effects: enhance cards, create jokers, add money/hands

Planets - Permanently upgrade specific poker hands (+chips & +mult)

👹 Boss Blinds

Every 3rd round, face a boss blind with special rules that make the game harder!

🎴 Deck Types

Choose your starting deck for unique bonuses: extra hands/discards, more money, cheaper jokers, extra joker slot, or starting tarots.
