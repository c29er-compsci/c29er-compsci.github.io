🎮 MORE GAMES! 🎮

Want more awesome games?

Tons of free games to play!

🎮 SINGLE PLAYER CONTROLS

WASD / ARROW KEYS - Move left/right
W / UP (on ground) - Jump
W / UP (in air with Cape) - Glide/Slow fall
W / UP (on rope) - Climb up
S / DOWN (on rope) - Climb down
SPACE (on rope) - Jump off rope
Q or CLICK - Attack with whip
SPACE - Throw bomb
R - Deploy rope
E - Pick up items, break crates, interact with NPCs/quests
ESC - Pause game / Close menus

👥 TWO PLAYER CONTROLS

PLAYER 1 (Brown - WASD Area):
WASD - Move
Q - Whip
E - Bomb
R - Rope
F - Interact (pick up, break crates, shop)
PLAYER 2 (Blue - Arrow Keys Area):
Arrow Keys - Move
/ (Slash) - Whip
. (Period) - Bomb
, (Comma) - Rope
ENTER - Interact (pick up, break crates, shop)
⚠️ SHARED: Health and key are shared! Each player has their own gold, bombs, and ropes!
🚪 BOTH PLAYERS must reach the door to advance!

💰 COLLECTIBLES

💰 Gold - Small coins ($100) & large coins ($500)
💎 Gems - Sapphire ($100), Emerald ($200), Ruby ($300)
📦 Crates - Press E to break for gold inside
🔑 Key - Required to unlock the exit door!

🛒 SHOP ITEMS

💣 Bombs ($200) - Destroy terrain & enemies
🪢 Ropes ($200) - Reach high places
👢 Spike Boots ($800) - Walk on spikes, stomp enemies (lasts 2 levels)
👓 Spectacles ($700) - Reveal hidden cyan gold coins! (lasts 2 levels)
🧭 Compass ($500) - Golden arrow points to exit door (lasts 2 levels)
🦸 Cape ($900) - Hold W/UP while jumping to glide slowly! (lasts 2 levels)

📜 QUEST SYSTEM

🧙 Quest Giver - Look for the glowing NPC with ! above their head
📋 Quests - Kill enemies, collect gold/gems, destroy blocks, break crates
💰 Rewards - Complete quests to earn bonus gold!
📊 Progress - Track your quest progress in the bottom-left corner

⚠️ DANGERS

🔺 Spikes - Instant death (unless you have spike boots)
➡️ Arrow Traps - Shoot arrows when you're nearby
🦇 Bat - Flies erratically, chases player
🐍 Snake - Jumps at player, fast movement
🕷️ Spider - Crawls on ceilings, drops on player
🧔 Caveman - Throws rocks from distance
💀 Skeleton - Reassembles after death!

🎯 GOAL

🔑 Find the golden KEY hidden in each level
🚪 With the key, enter the EXIT DOOR to advance!
📍 Complete 4 sublevels to reach the next world
🌍 Worlds: Cave → Jungle → Ice → Volcano (then loops)

💾 SAVE SYSTEM

💾 Press ESC to pause, then click "SAVE GAME"
📂 Click "LOAD GAME" from main menu to continue
🗑️ Click "DELETE SAVE" to start fresh
⚡ Game auto-saves when you advance levels!
